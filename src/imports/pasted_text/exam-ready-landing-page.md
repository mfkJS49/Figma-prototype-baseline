Create a complete, premium, modern SaaS landing page and product UI for an AI EdTech platform called **Exam Ready**.

The product helps students find out whether they are actually ready for an exam.

The core experience is:

**Upload your course → Take an AI-powered oral exam → Get an Exam Readiness Score → Discover your weak topics → Improve and retake the exam.**

The website must look like a polished, funded startup product, not a generic AI-generated template.

## DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a premium SaaS / EdTech visual identity inspired by the quality and simplicity of modern products such as Linear, Raycast, Perplexity, Stripe and high-end Apple product pages.

The interface should feel:

* premium
* intelligent
* young but serious
* trustworthy
* minimal
* highly polished
* modern
* mobile-first
* appropriate for students aged approximately 15–25

Do NOT make it childish.

Do NOT use excessive gradients.

Do NOT make it look like a generic school website.

Do NOT overload the interface with illustrations.

Use plenty of whitespace, beautiful typography, subtle shadows, rounded cards and refined micro-interactions.
